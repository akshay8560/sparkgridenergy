Solar Batteries for Homes & Businesses

Store your solar energy and take control of your power usage with a subsidised Solar Battery system. Whether you’re a homeowner or a business, you can reduce reliance on the grid, cut your energy bills, and enjoy reliable backup power — all with the support of government rebates.

  • Store excess solar power and reduce grid dependence.
  • Use more self-generated energy during peak tariff periods.
  • Improve energy independence and optional backup power.
Eligible solar batteries under the STC scheme must be between 5 kWh and 100 kWh nominal capacity, and STCs can only be claimed on the first 50 kWh of usable capacity. Backup capability depends on the battery model, inverter, switchboard setup and backup circuit design.
